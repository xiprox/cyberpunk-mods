# Vehicle Customizer

A mod for Cyberpunk 2077 in progress: an in-world mechanic shop where V buys a car or bike in a style and customizes its parts, paints and add-ons from any car, with co-op partners seeing it on NightCoop.
[UX.md](UX.md) is the experience it aims for; [RECORD.md](RECORD.md) says what we found and decided, and where things stand; [SHOPS.md](SHOPS.md) lists garages in the game that could become the shop.

- `mod/` is the mod itself, laid out like the game folder: the engine (`r6/scripts/VehicleCustomizer`), the catalogs, the shared wheel list and pools of copies (`r6/tweaks/vehiclecustomizer`, generated) and the twin looks (`archive/pc/mod/vehiclecustomizer/vehiclecustomizer.archive` and `.xl`, generated). The customization screen for the co-op test (`Shop.reds`, `Screen.reds`) opens with F7: a native panel beside the car with style, parts, wheels, paint, add-ons, service and garage tabs. It's a stand-in until there's a shop in the world. `tools/install.ps1` installs `mod/` and `dev/` with the game closed; the crew gets the mod's three folders through the log collector's Mods tab (RECORD.md says which).
- `tool-models/` makes the held tools: `make-tools.py` (run with `python -I`) reads the Lexington's files out of the game, puts in the meshes from `blockout.py` (Blender, headless) and writes `vehiclecustomizer_tools.archive` and its `.xl` into `mod/`. What it reads and makes stays in `tool-models/work/`, out of git.
- `catalog/` makes the generated parts: `make-catalogs.py` writes every car and bike's catalog from the game's own vehicle records (dumped by a test run) and its files, with summaries in `catalog/out/`; `make-twins.py` builds the twin-look archive. Run them with `python -I`; they read with the repo's readers (`../../tools/rdar.py`, `../../tools/pkg.py`) and need `experiments/areas/game-files/data/parts.json` and WolvenKit.
- `dev/` is the development harness, installed with the mod: a CET window over the engine ("Vehicle Customizer (dev)": style, paints, parts, wheels per axle with size, width and rim paint, add-ons with coarse and fine moves, repair, rebuild, verify, spawn or call a pool copy; and a "Shop scouting" window for visiting the places in SHOPS.md), and, only while a run's flag is set, a test runner, a main-menu auto-continue and a lock on saving. `tools/run-autotest.ps1 -Job <job>` runs one test in the game with nobody at the PC and keeps its report, pictures and the game's logs in `dev/runs/`; jobs are `suite` (the engine on a Hella pool copy, start to finish), `sweep` and `sweep_b` (every catalog in turn, with two different sets of choices), `wheels` (sets from the shared list, scaled and per axle), `repair` (dents, lost parts and a repair), `persist_save` then `persist_check` (a build and a garage unlock through a quicksave and a restart, cleaned up and quicksaved again at the end), `prepare` (copy 1 built and called to V, saving unlocked, the game left open for the user, with `-KeepGameOpen`) and `dump` (every vehicle record, for the catalog). `tools/contact-sheet.ps1 -Run <run folder>` puts a run's close-ups on labelled sheets.
- `test/` is the earlier in-game test (a CET window, TweakXL copies, a redscript service, an ArchiveXL archive), superseded by `mod/`; the first run of the mod moved it out of the game into `test/removed-from-game/`, its TweakXL copies too since the afternoon of 2026-11-06.
- `../../tools/check-scripts.ps1 -Mod vehicle-customizer` compiles the redscript of `mod/`, `dev/` and `test/` beside the installed mods without touching the game.
- `experiments/areas/game-files/` reads the game's archives with no modding tools, through the repo's readers in `../../tools/` (`rdar.py` for archives, with the game's own compression DLL, and `pkg.py` for CR2W files and packages). `run-all.ps1` runs every probe and saves dated output to `results/`; `sectors.py` and `garages.py` read the world's sectors to find garages.
